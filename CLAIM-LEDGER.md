## Publication gate

Before an external presentation is exported:

1. Confirm the review date on every visible claim note is still current.
2. Obtain owner approval for internal pilot, workforce, finance, and spend claims.
3. Add survey sample size and field dates for instructional AI percentages or remove the percentages.
4. Treat plans as targets unless completion evidence exists.
5. Never add potential reach, enabled accounts, active users, and trained participants together.
