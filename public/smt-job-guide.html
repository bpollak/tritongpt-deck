6:25 AM, weekdays · Pacific time

Morning calendar and email snapshot

Give me a quick view of the day before I open several applications.

What it reads
A fresh Microsoft 365 calendar and email snapshot, plus selected follow-up tracking.
What it does
A script ranks meetings and email highlights, assembles a dated briefing, and formats a short version for delivery. This particular job uses scripted rules rather than an open-ended agent run.
What it produces
A dated calendar/email briefing and a concise delivered summary.
What I still review
I assess what actually deserves attention. A meeting ranking or suggested talking point is a preparation aid.
Illustrative example
I can see the day’s key meetings and recent correspondence together instead of reconstructing them separately.

7:00 AM, daily · Pacific time

Harness daily meeting brief

Help me walk into a meeting with the relevant history already assembled.

What it reads
Live calendar entries, prior meeting notes, relevant work context, and recorded follow-through. The collector can also research relevant background.
What it does
The collector prepares one card per meeting: context, my follow-through, proposed agenda, and desired outcome. The delivery process checks freshness and attempts the Harness chat and email separately.
What it produces
A dated chat in the daily-briefing project and the same briefing in work email. Receipts help prevent duplicate deliveries during retries.
What I still review
I check the cited evidence and decide what belongs on the actual agenda. The brief does not change calendar events or perform the actions it finds.
Illustrative example
Before a recurring project meeting, I can review what we last discussed and which of my follow-ups remain open.

Every four hours; manual sorting remains available · Pacific time

Inbox priority sorting

Make the messages that need my attention easier to find.

What it reads
Work email content and metadata, together with configured priority rules.
What it does
Campus TritonAI classification and local rules estimate whether the message asks something of me and how urgently I need to respond. The system applies Outlook priority categories.
What it produces
Messages labeled Priority 1 · Today or Priority 2 · This week, with decision reasons available in the tool.
What I still review
I can correct the ranking. A priority score is a judgment under uncertainty, not a guarantee that a message is urgent.
Illustrative example
A direct request with a near deadline should be easier to spot than an informational announcement.

8:10 AM and 3:10 PM, weekdays · Pacific time

Work email reply drafts

Reduce the effort of starting a reply to important messages I have not answered.

What it reads
Recent work inbox messages, their conversation history, Sent Items, existing drafts, and relevant professional context.
What it does
The scheduled scan looks back 14 days and scans up to 50 inbox messages. It skips noise, acknowledgments, threads already answered, and threads with an existing draft. It selects high-scoring candidates and creates at most three reply drafts per run, then checks wording quality.
What it produces
Reviewable reply drafts in Exchange, plus a report of created drafts and skipped candidates.
What I still review
I check the facts, proposed commitments, wording, and recipients before sending. This job never sends email.
Illustrative example
A request that deserves a response arrives with a short draft grounded in the thread; an unknown answer stays something I need to confirm.

6:15 PM, weekdays · Pacific time

Meeting debrief and action review

Keep useful meeting follow-through from disappearing into notes.

What it reads
Available meeting notes/transcripts and the existing confirmed action list. The action review looks back over three days.
What it does
One step writes a dated debrief and structured extracts. A second separates confirmed actions from explicit requests addressed to me that still need review. It excludes casual statements that do not establish a confirmed task.
What it produces
A meeting debrief, structured extracts, and a concise action-review report.
What I still review
I confirm ownership and intended commitments. The job is instructed not to create task-board items from transcript heuristics.
Illustrative example
“Could Brett review this?” can become a review candidate; it should not silently turn into an accepted assignment.

7:15 PM, daily · Pacific time

Work session recap ingestion

Carry useful decisions and corrections from one agent-assisted work session into the next.

What it reads
Pending recaps of coding and work sessions, including completed artifacts, explicit corrections, and open follow-ups.
What it does
The job selects durable professional facts, adds dated decisions, and updates relevant reference pages. When facts conflict, it prefers the newer dated evidence and records the discrepancy. It marks recaps as processed to avoid ingesting the same recap repeatedly.
What it produces
Updated professional reference material and a short ingestion summary.
What I still review
I correct inaccurate or outdated context. The instructions exclude credentials and sensitive personal information.
Illustrative example
A naming correction or a decision about an artifact can remain available when I return to that work next week.

7:35 PM, weekdays · Pacific time

Nightly context consolidation

Connect the day’s separate reports so useful context is available later.

What it reads
Available briefings, meeting debriefs, evening summaries, opportunity research, and work artifacts from the day.
What it does
The job extracts decisions, commitments, and open follow-ups, then appends dated context and structured records. It is instructed to keep working when some inputs are missing and to preserve existing content.
What it produces
A consolidated daily record and updated professional reference material.
What I still review
I correct the record where necessary. A consolidated statement should remain traceable to the source artifacts; aggregation does not make an inference certain.
Illustrative example
A project decision discussed in a meeting and clarified during a later work session can be retrieved together.

8:00 PM, weekdays · Pacific time

Team knowledge proposals

Offer useful updates to shared team knowledge without directly rewriting the team’s established pages.

What it reads
Selected opportunities, decisions, and commitments extracted from daily work outputs.
What it does
A script prepares up to five proposals per night, including source references and action items, and can commit and push them to the team’s proposal area.
What it produces
Candidate updates in the shared proposal area, separate from established team pages.
What I still review
A person reviews a proposal before copying approved content into the relevant shared page. The proposal itself is visible to the team, so its content still needs an appropriate sharing boundary.
Illustrative example
A proposed update to a project decision can wait for the page owner’s review instead of automatically becoming the team’s stated position.

9:00 AM, weekdays · Pacific time

Team proposal reminder

Bring pending knowledge proposals back to someone’s attention.

What it reads
Proposal files currently waiting in the team’s proposal area.
What it does
A script lists up to 12 proposals and their age, then reminds the reviewer to copy approved material to the target page.
What it produces
A short reminder listing pending proposals. If none exist, the script has nothing to report.
What I still review
The reviewer accepts, corrects, or rejects each proposal. This job does not approve or merge a proposal.
Illustrative example
A useful proposal prepared overnight appears in the morning review queue instead of being forgotten.

6:35 AM, daily · Pacific time

Daily AI Digest

Keep up with relevant AI developments and turn the research into a useful public edition.

What it reads
A curated RSS feed, supplementary web research, relevant interests, and a list of recently published article URLs.
What it does
The job selects one to seven qualifying stories, checks source links and dates, excludes recent repeats, writes the edition, and follows the website publication process. It is instructed to publish fewer items rather than fill the edition with weak or invented material.
What it produces
A dated AI Digest edition on the personal website, with source links and commentary.
What I still review
I can inspect the published edition and its links. Recurring publication has a defined scope; that permission does not extend to unrelated public statements.
Illustrative example
The public October 19 edition is the concrete output shown in the deck. It can be read without access to private systems.

7:00 AM, daily · Pacific time

Campus AI opportunity scan

Turn broad higher-education research into a specific idea worth evaluating.

What it reads
Recent higher-education use cases, known work priorities, and an inventory of potential campus data sources.
What it does
The job performs up to three searches and scores candidates on recency, impact, relevance, data feasibility, and urgency. It selects the strongest candidate above its threshold, identifies required data and access considerations, and adds a nonduplicate idea to the opportunity pipeline.
What it produces
A dated opportunity note and a concise proposed use case, or “No strong signal today.”
What I still review
I decide whether the idea deserves investigation, a sponsor, and an owner. The score does not establish a business case or grant data access.
Illustrative example
The useful output describes what a person would submit, what a proposed tool would do, and what the person would receive back.

Every four hours · Pacific time

Vendor document watcher

Notice when an important new commercial document arrives without repeatedly checking the inbox myself.

What it reads
A bounded scan of recent work email for defined agreement and commercial-document triggers.
What it does
The watcher checks for a new matching document and stages its subject, sender, date, and attachment names for follow-on research. If it finds no trigger, it stays quiet.
What it produces
A short alert identifying a document ready for research.
What I still review
A person decides whether to begin analysis. The watcher does not assess the terms itself, accept an agreement, or make a purchasing decision.
Illustrative example
A newly received order form can prompt a research review without the watcher attempting to negotiate or approve it.

Current vendor-watcher instructions, inspected October 20, 2026. Private vendors and commercial terms are intentionally omitted.

Every 72 hours · Pacific time

TritonAI roadmap review

Keep the public roadmap consistent with evidence about what has actually shipped or changed.

What it reads
Current roadmap data, recent decisions and project records, primary meeting evidence where needed, and public service pages.
What it does
The job compares the evidence with the roadmap, makes supported changes within its standing authorization, runs repository checks, and verifies the actual public result. Unsupported new commitments stay questions for review.
What it produces
Supported roadmap corrections and a private review record; no cosmetic freshness update when there is no substantive change.
What I still review
I resolve uncertain commitments and scope questions. Standing authorization covers this roadmap maintenance, not unrelated site or policy changes.
Illustrative example
A supported delivery can move from a tentative description to an accurate current status; an announced date alone does not prove delivery.

6:00 AM, Sundays · Pacific time

Weekly decision brief

Spend my attention on calls that require my judgment rather than another list of status updates.

What it reads
The week’s briefings, meeting debriefs, and evening summaries.
What it does
The job keeps decisions I own, explains the stakes and consequences of delay, lists deadlines and missing facts, and ranks up to seven items. It is instructed not to invent deadlines or decisions.
What it produces
A concise decision brief. If fewer than three genuine decisions qualify, the job can remain silent.
What I still review
I make the decision, seek missing evidence, or explicitly defer it. The job cannot approve a course of action for me.
Illustrative example
A question with an approaching deadline and unresolved evidence appears as a decision to prepare for, rather than a generic follow-up task.
